Connecting

This page talks to the synth with Web Bluetooth, which needs HTTPS.
Windows and Android — Chrome or Edge, works as-is.
iPhone and iPad — Safari has no Web Bluetooth. Open this page in Bluefy, a free WebBLE browser on the App Store.
Power the synth, choose Connect, and pick SynthSizer. If it is not listed, try Scan all, or power-cycle the synth and toggle Bluetooth off and on.
