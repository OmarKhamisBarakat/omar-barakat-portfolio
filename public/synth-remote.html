Connecting

Needs Web Bluetooth over HTTPS.
Windows / Android: Chrome or Edge — works as-is.

Power the synth, tap Connect, pick Synth.
Not listed? Tap Scan all to show every nearby BLE device. If it still hides, power-cycle the synth and toggle Bluetooth off/on — phones cache old advertisements.
